Our Story

We're Not Just
Bubble Tea.

Buba Junction started with a single dream — to bring Nashik a bubble tea experience that truly stands out. Not just another café, but a destination where every cup is crafted with intention.

We source premium tea leaves, use real fruit purees, and hand-roll every single tapioca pearl. Because if you're going to sip something, it might as well be extraordinary.
