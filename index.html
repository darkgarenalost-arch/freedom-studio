FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a website take to build? +
Single-page websites are typically ready in 3–5 business days. Multi-page business websites take 7–10 days. Premium websites with advanced animations take 10–14 days. We always share a realistic timeline on your discovery call.
Do you run the ads or just set them up? +
Both options are available. We can fully manage your Meta/Facebook/Instagram ad campaigns on a monthly retainer, or set up the campaigns and hand them over to you with full training. Most clients prefer the managed option so we handle optimisation continuously.
What kind of businesses do you work with? +
We work with local businesses, restaurants, real estate agents, startups, e-commerce brands, personal brands, coaches, and service businesses. If you're a business that needs more customers — we can help.
Can I see the website before I pay? +
Yes. We provide a complete preview before final payment. You only pay 50% to start the project and 50% after you're fully satisfied with the result. No risk on your end.
Do you offer revisions? +
Absolutely. Every project includes unlimited revisions until you're 100% happy. We don't stop until the work is exactly what you need.

Freedom Studio was born from a simple frustration: too many great businesses were invisible online, while inferior ones dominated because they had better marketing.

We built Freedom Studio to change that — to give local businesses, restaurants, coaches, and startups access to the same level of premium design and performance marketing that big brands take for granted.

Every project we take on is a mission. Not just to make something beautiful, but to make something that actually works — that brings in more customers, more bookings, more sales.
